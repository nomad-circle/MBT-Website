0001 BMT Website – Recommended Content
0002 The main purpose of the website is to introduce BMT properly to international defense companies, especially American and European manufacturers looking for a Saudi partner. The website should be simple, professional and clear about what BMT can provide in Saudi Arabia.
0003 1. Homepage / Introduction
0004 BMT – Your Strategic Partner in the Saudi Defense Market
0005 Suggested introduction:
0006 BMT is a Saudi defense and security company established to connect leading international defense manufacturers with opportunities in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Backed by an established Saudi holding group and led by an experienced management team, BMT provides market representation, business development, localization support and in-country services to international partners seeking sustainable growth in the Saudi market.
0007 Main areas to show clearly on the homepage: International Representation | Market Access | Localization | In-Country Support

0105 Overall Positioning
0106 The website should position BMT as a Saudi defense and security company providing international manufacturers with market representation, localization support and long-term in-country capabilities in the Kingdom.
0107 Supporting message: Backed by an established Saudi holding group and led by an experienced management team, BMT was established to build strategic partnerships between leading international defense companies and the Saudi market.
